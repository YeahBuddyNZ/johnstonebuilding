We build a small number of homes each year, so the ones we do build carry our name properly.

Johnstone Building works with architects, designers and homeowners across Auckland on projects where the finish is the point — where a 3mm reveal, a level line across a room, and a door that closes with weight are what separate a good house from a great one.

Hayden Johnstone leads every job personally. That means one point of contact from first site visit to final handover, a programme you can plan your life around, and a build site you would be happy to walk a client through unannounced.
