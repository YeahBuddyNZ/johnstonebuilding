Restoration & extension · Titirangi

Kohu Road Residence

A 1920s villa repaired properly at the front, and opened into a quiet new pavilion at the back.

Type
Restoration & extension
Location
Titirangi
Completed
2023
Design
In collaboration with the owners' designer
Scale
Three bedrooms plus studio, 210m²
Role
Main contractor

Restoration and extension in one contract, where the join between the two is the only part anyone will ever notice.

The original villa had been altered twice before, badly. We took the front rooms back to the framing, replaced the rotten bearers and rebuilt the verandah with profiles milled to match the surviving originals rather than the nearest stock item.

At the rear, a new pavilion picks up the villa's floor level and ceiling height exactly, so you walk through the junction without stepping or ducking. The link between the two is glazed, low and deliberately quiet.

The detail

Where the time went.

Matching a hundred-year-old scriber profile is a two-day job before a single board is fixed: take a section off the best surviving piece, have a cutter ground, run a sample, check it in place at the same time of day the light hits it.
