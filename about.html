About

A builder, not
a building company.

Johnstone Building is led by Hayden Johnstone. The list of live projects is kept short on purpose, because the alternative is a site you visit rather than a site you run.

The practice

The pride we take in our work and attention to detail are paramount. Construction is our passion.

That line has been on the front of everything we do for years, and it is not marketing — it is the filter we run every decision through. If a detail can be done properly or done quickly, it gets done properly, and the programme is built to allow for that from day one.

In practice it means a small team who have worked together long enough to know each other's standard, a tight group of subcontractors we return to job after job, and a preference for being involved early enough to give the architect and the client honest buildability feedback while it still costs nothing to change.

What we hold to

Four things that do not get traded away.

01

Detail before speed

Shadow gaps, reveals and junctions are set out before the trade starts, not negotiated once the material is on the wall.

02

A site you would show off

Swept, safe, materials stacked and protected. A tidy site is the cheapest quality-control system there is.

03

Straight answers

If something in the drawings will not work, or will cost more than you think, you hear it immediately — not in a variation later.

04

Still here afterwards

Timber moves, houses settle. We come back and sort it out, because our name is on the letterbox as much as yours.
