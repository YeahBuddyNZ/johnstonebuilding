Services

Everything we do,
we do to the same
standard.

We take on a limited number of projects each year. It is the only way to run a build where the person who priced it is also the person on site when the hard details get resolved.

Licensed Building Practitioner Fixed programme Weekly written reporting Itemised pricing One point of contact Post-handover support

01 — New builds

Architectural new builds

Full delivery from earthworks through to handover. We work directly to the architect's drawings, flag conflicts before they become variations, and hold the detail through every trade that walks onto the site.

Includes
Consent coordination, earthworks, foundations, framing, closing-in, interior finishing, landscaping coordination
Typical run
9–18 months depending on scale and site access

02 — Renovations

Renovations & extensions

Structural alterations, rear additions and full reconfigurations. The measure of a good renovation is that nobody can tell where the old work stops — floor levels line through, ceiling heights resolve, and the new roof meets the old one properly.

We are comfortable working in occupied homes, with dust control, staged access and a site left safe every night.

03 — Restoration

Character restoration

Villas and bungalows deserve repair rather than replacement. We match existing profiles, replace rotten scribers and facings rather than filling them, and resolve the weathertightness problems that caused the damage in the first place.

Where original material can be saved, we save it. Where it cannot, the replacement is milled to match.

04 — Fitouts

Interior & commercial fitouts

Showrooms, studios and offices where the space itself is the argument for the standard of work inside it. Recent work includes turning a timber distributor's new premises into an elegant showroom for its own products.

Out-of-hours programming, tenancy coordination and staged handovers where trading has to continue.

05 — Management

Project management

Programme, budget, consents and subcontractors held in one place. You get a weekly written update covering what was done, what is next, what is on order and where the budget sits — short enough that you will actually read it.

Available as a standalone service for clients running their own build with separate trades.

Good to know

The questions we get asked most.

Where do you work?
Across Hamilton and the wider Waikato — Cambridge, Te Awamutu, Tamahere, Raglan and the surrounding districts. Further afield for the right project.
Do you price plans?
Yes. Send drawings and a specification and you will get an itemised estimate with allowances shown clearly as allowances.
Do you work with our architect?
Always. We prefer to be involved early enough to give buildability and cost feedback while the design can still absorb it.
How many jobs at once?
Deliberately few. It is what makes the level of supervision — and the finish — possible.
