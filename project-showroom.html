The Timber Showroom

A timber distributor's new premises in industrial Auckland, turned into an elegant showroom for its own products.

Type
Commercial fitout
Location
Auckland
Completed
2023
Design
Client brief, developed on site
Scale
Showroom and office, 260m²
Role
Main contractor

When the client sells timber, the fitout is the sample. Every surface had to be a product they could point at and quote.

The brief was straightforward and unforgiving: build the office so that the timber does the talking. That meant a lot of species in one room, and a discipline about where each one starts and stops so it reads as a considered palette rather than a display rack.

Structure was left exposed and painted out, so the eye goes to the joinery. Lighting was set out against the timber, not the ceiling grid, and the reception face was built as a single unbroken run with no visible fixings.

The detail

Where the time went.

The showroom wall carries eleven species side by side. They were laid out full-size on the floor first, shuffled over two days, and only then fixed — because on paper the sequence looked right and in the room it did not.
